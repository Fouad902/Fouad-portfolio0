// About

Hey, I'm Fouad

I'm a backend developer based in Giza, Egypt, focused on building production-grade systems with Node.js, NestJS, and TypeScript.

I got into backend development because I love the invisible work — the APIs that just work, the databases that scale, the authentication that doesn't leak. I enjoy thinking about edge cases, clean architecture, and how to make code that other developers can read six months later.

Currently, I contribute to Inboxex CRM, a production platform used by clients across Egypt and international markets. Before that, I interned at Misr Insurance working on Spring Boot and frontend features.

Outside of backend work, I also do AI content creation — prompt engineering, generative imagery, and visual storytelling. It keeps my creative side alive and taught me a completely different kind of "systems thinking".
